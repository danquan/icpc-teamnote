\subsection*{Distance from a point to a line}
Given a point $P(x_0,y_0)$ and a line $d:\ ax+by+c=0$:
\[
\mathrm{d}(P,d)=\frac{|a x_0+b y_0+c|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}.
\]

\subsection*{Distance between two lines}
Two parallel lines: $d_1:\ ax+by+c_1=0$, $d_2:\ ax+by+c_2=0$:
\[
\mathrm{d}(d_1,d_2)=\frac{|c_1-c_2|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}.
\]
